    Checking which voices this device has…

    • There are three races a day: Numbers, English and 中文. Each has 4 short laps and wins its own trophy. The same date and class always give the same race, so siblings can compare.
    • Kart counts to 5, adds within 5 and uses capital letters. Junior counts to 10, adds within 10 and uses small letters. Pro goes to 20, adds across ten, and reads short words like "cat" and Chinese words like 苹果.
    • Read "+" aloud as "and … more makes". Say "3 red cars and 2 blue cars make 5 cars" before you say "3 plus 2 equals 5".
    • In Count laps, tap each car once. It shows its number and says it aloud. Let your child do the tapping.
    • The app reads out each question in English and says Chinese words in Mandarin. The voice says letter names (bee, see). Please say the letter sounds (b, k) yourself as you play.
    • Pinyin is shown under Chinese words so you can read them along. In Listen laps the pinyin stays hidden until the right answer is found.
    • Wrong answers just fade out. Let your child try again. In the Grand Prix a missed question comes back later in the same race, and every race ends on the podium.
    • Trophies, the streak and best stars are saved in this browser on this device only.
